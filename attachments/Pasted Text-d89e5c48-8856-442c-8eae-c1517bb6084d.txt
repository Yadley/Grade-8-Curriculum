Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project Title: African Literature Digital Repository & Supplementary Resource Hub
Thesis Project: Grade 8 English Teachers’ Literary Selections, Strategies and Challenges in Teaching African Literature: Inputs for Supplementary Based Materials
Researcher: Tarisai Tara Mabuto, MAEd Candidate (Language and Literature), Mariano Marcos State University
Advisory Committee Chair: Romel A. Pascua, Ph.D.
Target Delivery Date: October 13, 2026 (Live Landing Page MVP)
Primary Platform: Responsive Web Landing Page (Google Sites / Webflow / Static HTML via GitHub Pages)
1. Executive Summary & Purpose
The African Literature Digital Repository serves as the developmental output and supplementary instructional artifact mandated by the thesis title suffix, "Inputs for Supplementary Based Materials." The website provides Grade 8 English secondary educators in the Schools Division of Laoag City (and across the Philippines) with an open-access, curriculum-aligned repository.
The hub hosts a curated 10-piece African literary corpus—balanced across gender (feminism/masculinity), geographic regions, historical eras, and non-racial themes—alongside ready-to-download Lesson Exemplars, vocabulary-unlocking scaffolds, and pedagogical strategy matrices based on the Carter & Long models under the Revised K to 10 Curriculum.
2. Target Audience & Personas
 * Primary Users: Grade 8 English Public Secondary High School Teachers in Laoag City implementing Afro-Asian literature.
   * Pain Points Addressed: Severe shortage of curriculum-aligned learning packages, lack of contextual background for African literature, out-of-pocket printing expenses, and limited class periods (45–60 mins).
 * Secondary Users: Secondary Head Teachers, Master Teachers, English Department Coordinators, and Curriculum Evaluators.
 * Tertiary Users: Graduate Advisory Committee (Dr. Romel Pascua, Dr. Winicel May Ancheta, Dr. Jahnese Asuncion, Prof. Jeanette Dials) assessing academic deliverables and developmental validity.
3. Product Goals & Core Objectives
 * Host Curated Corpus: Provide complete, accessible texts or annotated excerpts for the 10 selected African literary pieces across 5 distinct genres (5 poems, 2 short stories, 1 essay, 1 novel, 1 speculative fiction).
 * Explicit Selection Criteria: Document the pedagogical, thematic, and developmental rationale governing text selection for Philippine secondary learners.
 * Instructional Support: Provide downloadable lesson exemplars, graphic organizers, and pre-reading vocabulary tasks mapped to the Carter & Long literature teaching models.
 * Intercultural Bridging: Explicitly connect African sociocultural dynamics to Filipino communal values, family structures, and lived realities.
 * Strict Curricular Compliance: Align strictly with the Revised K to 10 Curriculum guidelines without any obsolete or prohibited terminology.

6. Functional & Non-Functional Requirements
6.1 Functional Requirements
 * Text Previews & Modals: Clicking any literary piece opens a modal or sub-panel displaying the author biography, historical context, full text/approved excerpt, and core vocabulary terms.
 * Direct Asset Downloads: Functional download links for PDF and Word format Lesson Exemplars and Graphic Organizers.
 * Responsive Filtering: Instant JavaScript/tag-based filtering on the literary grid by genre (Poetry, Short Story, Essay, Novel, Speculative).
 * Feedback / Contact Form: Embedded lightweight form for teachers to submit comments on classroom usability.
6.2 Non-Functional Requirements
 * Mobile Responsiveness: Flawless rendering on mobile devices (Android/iOS) given that Philippine teachers frequently access materials via smartphones.
 * Low-Bandwidth Optimization: Fast loading speeds (< 2 seconds) even under standard 3G/4G public school connections; images compressed in WebP format; clean vector SVG icons.
 * Strict Terminology Guardrails: 100% adherence to the directive to exclude prohibited terms; all curriculum alignments must reference the Revised K to 10 Curriculum.
 * Accessibility: High-contrast color scheme adhering to WCAG 2.1 AA standards; legible typography (inter/system sans-serif fonts).
7. Visual Design & Style System
 * Color Palette:
   * Primary Brand: Terracotta Ochre (#C85A17) — reflective of African earth and cultural heritage.
   * Secondary Accent: Deep Forest Green (#1E4D2B) — symbolizing ecological vitality and growth.
   * Neutral Background: Off-White / Sand (#FDFBF7) — easy on the eyes for extended reading.
   * Typography Base: Charcoal (#222222) for primary body text; Warm Grey (#555555) for metadata.
 * Typography Hierarchy:
   * Headings: Serif with character (e.g., Merriweather or Playfair Display) for literary authority.
   * Body & UI: Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Roboto) for maximum legibility on mobile screens.
